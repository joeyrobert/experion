Moves

Every engine runs in your browser. Experion and Fruit are compiled to WebAssembly and run on a single thread; CeruleanJS is plain JavaScript with its opening book. Pick any pairing, including engine against engine. The desktop Experion, using several threads, is stronger. connects native engines on your own computer (for example Crafty) through tools/engine-bridge.mjs.
